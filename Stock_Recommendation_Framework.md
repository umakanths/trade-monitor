## What I Analyze For Each Recommendation

### 1. **Technical Analysis**
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)**: Looking for 50-70 range (momentum without overbought)
- **MACD**: Bullish crossovers for entry signals
- **Moving Averages**: Price above 50-day and 200-day for uptrend confirmation
- **Volume**: Rising volume confirms breakout strength
- **Support/Resistance**: Key levels for entry and stop-loss placement

### 2. **Historical Trends**
- **52-Week Performance**: Context for valuation and trend strength
- **Recent Catalysts**: Earnings surprises, product launches, analyst upgrades
- **Sector Rotation**: Which sectors are hot (AI, energy, healthcare, etc.)
- **Momentum Comparison**: Which stocks outperformed peers this month

### 3. **Fundamental Catalysts**
- **Earnings Dates**: Buy before expected beats, sell on disappointments
- **News Events**: Product announcements, acquisitions, regulatory changes
- **Management Changes**: New leadership or strategic pivots
- **Industry Trends**: Tailwinds vs headwinds (e.g., AI boom, shipping rates)

### 4. **Risk Assessment**
- **Volatility**: Beta rating (higher = riskier, bigger swings)
- **Position Sizing**: How much of your €25-50 to allocate
- **Stop-Loss Placement**: Where to cut losses (usually -8% to -10%)
- **Risk/Reward Ratio**: Expected profit vs downside risk
